==================================================
BACKGROUND
==================================================

Audit #228 identified two potential issues:

1. TripInviteToken acceptance appears to check:
   is_active
   but may not enforce:
   expires_at

2. Any logged-in user who possesses the token URL may be able to accept and join the trip.

It is currently unclear whether this second behavior is intentional product design or an authorization gap.

We need the exact current behavior before changing anything.
