Investigate BL-123 — BUG: Normalize and validate wishlist destination IDs.

PLAN / INVESTIGATION ONLY.

Do not modify application code.
Do not modify templates.
Do not modify schema or migrations.
Do not create a commit.

GOAL

Determine the exact current wishlist write/read behavior and produce a build-ready fix plan for BL-123.

The known problem is:

- wishlist endpoints can store duplicate destination IDs
- invalid destination types may be accepted
- inactive resorts may be accepted
- these issues can distort:
  - the 15-item limit
  - wishlist counts
  - Ideas matching
  - Mountain social signals

The desired contract is:

- IDs are normalized and deduplicated before storage
- deduplication occurs before enforcing the 15-item maximum
- only valid active resort destinations may be persisted
- region records are not valid wishlist resorts
- inactive resorts are not valid wishlist resorts
- behavior is consistent across all wishlist mutation paths
- canonical User.wish_list_resorts behavior remains intact
