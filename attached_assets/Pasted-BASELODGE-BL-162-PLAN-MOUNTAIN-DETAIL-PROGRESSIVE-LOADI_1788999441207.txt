CONTEXT

The completed BL-162 investigation established:

- GET /mountain/<slug> currently performs the entire Mountain Detail social query graph before returning initial HTML.
- The route is authenticated.
- Core mountain content does NOT require reciprocal friends, friend trips, participant rows, friend records, contextual insights, friend wishlist/visit summaries, or availability overlaps.
- Existing social queries are already bulk-loaded and query-bounded.
- The performance opportunity is moving that entire social graph off the critical initial-render path, NOT redesigning or optimizing the social queries themselves.
- Existing privacy/visibility rules are correct and must remain unchanged.
- No migration, dependency, configuration, native, or privacy-contract change is required.

PRODUCT / UX DECISION

Deferred social loading should begin IMMEDIATELY after the core Mountain Detail DOM renders.

Do NOT use IntersectionObserver or viewport-triggered loading.

The intended experience is:

1. Core mountain content renders first.
2. A compact neutral social loading region is present below it.
3. The browser immediately requests the social fragment.
4. Social content appears when ready.
5. Empty social content hides the region.
6. Failed social loading leaves the core page fully usable and provides a bounded Retry action.
