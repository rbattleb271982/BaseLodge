==================================================
BL-140 HISTORY CONTRACT
==================================================

Canonical conventions:
docs/history-event-conventions.md

BL-87 must conform to them.

Important principles:

- history is private/domain-specific
- current wishlist membership remains product truth
- history never becomes authorization or current-state truth
- append event in same transaction as current-state mutation
- bounded event types and sources
- server-generated timezone-aware timestamps
- no arbitrary metadata/copied PII unless required
- account/privacy deletion erases protected subject history
- actor-only deletion anonymizes actor where applicable
- default no synthetic backfill
- unknown-before-cutover semantics
- deterministic duplicate/no-op handling
- no generalized event store

7. DUPLICATE / IDEMPOTENCY / CONCURRENCY
==================================================

Design for:

- two simultaneous remove requests

- bulk update vs single mutation
- duplicate request retries

No-op/retry = zero events

Use PostgreSQL concurrency semantics.

Do not rely solely on event-table uniqueness constraints to prevent legitimate repeated add/remove lifecycles.

removed
