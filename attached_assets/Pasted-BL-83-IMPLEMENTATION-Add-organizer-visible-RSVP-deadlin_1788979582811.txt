--------------------------------------------------
APPROVED BL-83 PRODUCT CONTRACT
--------------------------------------------------

Implement an OPTIONAL organizer-visible RSVP deadline on SkiTrip.

The deadline is passive/informational canonical trip data.

Approved semantics:

1. RSVP deadline is optional.
2. Organizer can add it.
3. Organizer can edit it.
4. Organizer can remove it.
5. Authorized Trip Detail viewers can see it.
6. Any valid calendar date is allowed.
7. Deadline MAY equal the trip start date.
8. Deadline MAY occur after the trip starts.
9. Deadline MAY already be in the past when created or edited.
10. Do NOT compare the deadline against trip dates or today's date.
11. Deadline does NOT enforce RSVP behavior.
12. Participants may continue changing RSVP state after the deadline.
13. Do NOT add RSVP deadline to trip creation in BL-83.
14. Deadline management happens from Trip Detail only.

Explicitly OUT OF SCOPE:

- reminders
- reminder scheduling
- push notifications
- email
- SMS
- lifecycle communications
- organizer follow-up
- Home actions
- deadline-driven recommendations
- Happening distribution
- participant action tracking
- RSVP enforcement
- analytics changes
- native iOS/Android work
- changes to legacy GroupTrip/TripGuest
- trip-creation deadline input
