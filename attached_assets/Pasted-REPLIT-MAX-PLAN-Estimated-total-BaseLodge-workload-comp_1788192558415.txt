TASK: BL-80 — ⚠️ USER DATA / SCHEMA — Preserve completed and cancelled trip history

Investigate and design only. DO NOT implement yet.

BL-80 goal from Notion:

Ensure past trips remain a reliable historical record rather than disappearing or losing important semantics after completion/cancellation.

Requirements:
- Define completed vs cancelled historical states.
- Preserve participant/date/trip references required for historical display.
- Do not duplicate the trip into a second historical object.
- Existing Trip Detail remains the canonical trip record.
- Respect deletion/privacy behavior.

USER DATA SAFETY GATE:
This changes retention/semantics of persisted trip/user data. Require compatibility review, migration/backfill decision, rollback strategy, tests, and post-change verification.

BL-140 HISTORY CONTRACT IS NOW CANONICAL

Repository documentation:
docs/history-event-conventions.md

BL-80 must conform to that contract.

Important principles:
- history is private/domain-specific
- existing current-state models remain authoritative
- history does not become authorization truth
- no generalized event store
- no synthetic backfill by default
- current-state mutation and event history should share a transaction
- bounded event/source vocabularies
- server-generated timezone-aware timestamps
- privacy subject deletion must erase/anonymize appropriately
- actor-only deletion should anonymize actor
- deterministic locking/concurrency where races are possible
- unknown-before-cutover semantics

2. DEFINE COMPLETED VS CANCELLED

Do not assume the answer.

COMPLETED

CANCELLED

- What historical fields must remain for Trip Detail?
