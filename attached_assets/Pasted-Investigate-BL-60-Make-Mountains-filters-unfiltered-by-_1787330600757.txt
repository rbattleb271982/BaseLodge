## BL-60 requirements to investigate

### Default Mountains behavior

The Mountains tab should:

* Open with the full unfiltered mountain list every time.
* Never automatically preselect the user's home state, country, pass, or another filter.
* Reset all filters whenever the user leaves the Mountains tab and returns, including during the same session.
* Not persist prior filter selections across tab visits or app launches.

### First-visit filter education

The first time an account visits Mountains:

* Highlight the entire filter area containing Country, State, and Pass.
* Show a tooltip/popover pointing to the overall filter area.
* Use this exact copy: **Filter mountains by state and pass.**
* The only explicit control should be an **X**.
* It can also dismiss when:

  * the user taps outside it,
  * it times out after a short period,
  * or the user interacts with a filter.
* Any dismissal method should permanently mark the education as seen.
* This state must persist at the **account level**, not just locally on the device.

### Permanent filter treatment

After the education treatment disappears:

* Keep the filter area slightly more noticeable than today.
* Use a subtle tinted background and light outline/container treatment.
* Do not make the filters look selected when none are active.
* Reuse the existing BaseLodge design system rather than introducing arbitrary new visual patterns.

### Active filters

When filters are active:

* Active controls should appear visually stronger than inactive controls.
* Show a small **Clear** action only when at least one filter is active.
* Clear should remove every active filter and immediately restore the full mountain list.
