## Finding 6 — Dismiss controls

The audit identified dismiss "×" controls that may fall below recommended mobile touch-target sizes, including:

* `.bl-happening-dismiss`
* `.bl-opp-dismiss`
* `.hc-dismiss-x`
* `.hc-idea-dismiss`

The audit recommends standardizing the interactive target to approximately:

* iOS: minimum 44×44pt
* Android: minimum 48×48dp

Important: we do **not** want the visible × glyph enlarged simply to achieve the target size. The goal is to enlarge the tappable area while preserving the existing visual treatment and card layout.

Investigate:

1. Every consumer-facing dismiss/close control in the current codebase, not just the four named above.
2. Current visible dimensions and actual tappable dimensions.
3. Which controls fall below relevant iOS, Android and WCAG target-size guidance.
4. Whether increasing padding/minimum dimensions would alter card layout, alignment or spacing.
5. Whether invisible/expanded hit areas can be used where appropriate.
6. Whether iOS and Android actually require different implementations in our Capacitor architecture or whether one safe cross-platform target can be used.
7. Whether any dismiss action is sufficiently consequential that Undo should be added.

Do not automatically add Undo. Report where it would and would not make sense.

## Finding 7 — Inline actions, buttons, chips and other interactive controls

The audit specifically identified examples including:

* `.td-edit-link`
* `.home-match-primary`
* `.fr-copy-link`
* `.fr-show-qr-btn`
* `.bl-btn-ghost`
* `.bl-chip`
* `.chip`

However, do **not** assume every visually small control needs to become a visibly 44–48px-tall component.

Inventory consumer-facing interactive controls whose actual tap area falls below appropriate mobile guidance.

For each type, distinguish between:

* Text links/actions
* Buttons
* Selection chips
* Filter chips
* Action pills
* Inline controls
* Controls contained within a larger tappable parent
* Controls where sufficient spacing or equivalent targeting may satisfy applicable accessibility guidance

Determine the safest way to enlarge the **interactive target** while preserving the current BaseLodge visual design and vertical rhythm.

Where padding would visibly distort the interface, investigate approaches such as expanded invisible hit areas, pseudo-elements, or padding combined with compensating margins.

Do **not** mechanically set every chip or inline action to `min-height: 44px`.
