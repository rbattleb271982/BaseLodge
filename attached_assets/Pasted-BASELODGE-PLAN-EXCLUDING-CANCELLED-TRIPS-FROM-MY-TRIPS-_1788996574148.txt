CONTEXT

A completed investigation established that BaseLodge organizer “delete” is actually a soft cancellation:

- Organizer cancel/delete sets SkiTrip.lifecycle_state = "cancelled"
- The SkiTrip row is intentionally retained
- My Trips History currently includes trips when:
  - end_date < today, OR
  - lifecycle_state is completed or cancelled
- Therefore cancelled trips appear under Past Trips
- The existing cancellation copy says the trip will be removed from the user’s profile and friends’ activity
- That copy conflicts with current History behavior

PRODUCT DECISION

Cancelled trips should NOT appear in My Trips Past Trips.

Past Trips should represent trips that genuinely became historical through their dates/completion, not trips the user cancelled.

Cancelled trips should remain fully retained in the database for lifecycle/history/integrity purposes.

Do not physically delete cancelled SkiTrip rows.
