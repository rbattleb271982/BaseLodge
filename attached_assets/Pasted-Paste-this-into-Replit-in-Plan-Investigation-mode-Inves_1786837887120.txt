> ## Observed behavior
>
> Richard suggested several people to Jon using the new Suggested Friends feature.
>
> Jon received the push notification correctly.
>
> However, when Jon tapped the push notification, the app opened to the **Home screen** instead of:
>
> `Friends → Suggested Friends`
>
> Jon then had to manually tap **Friends** and then **Suggested Friends**.
>
> The intended behavior is:
>
> **Tap Suggested Friends push → open `/friends?tab=suggested` directly.**
>
