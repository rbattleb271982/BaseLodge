==================================================
PURPOSE
==================================================

BL-85 is a cross-product privacy/intelligence investigation.

BaseLodge should not proactively aggregate, infer, rank, recommend, summarize, or notify based on private activity that the viewer is not authorized to see in a way that reveals or emphasizes that the viewer may have been excluded.

Example:

If several of a user's friends are participating in a PRIVATE Aspen trip that the viewer cannot see, BaseLodge must not use that hidden trip to generate intelligence such as:

- “Your friends are heading to Aspen.”
- “Aspen is popular with your friends.”
- “A lot of your friends are skiing this weekend.”
- a recommendation whose ranking is boosted because of that hidden trip
- a notification derived from that hidden activity

This does NOT mean BaseLodge must hide information the viewer is already authorized to see.

Public/authorized friend activity, authorized trip participation, shared availability, visible Wishlist information, visible pass information, and other existing authorized social signals may continue to contribute where the current product permits them.

The key question is:

CAN INFORMATION THAT IS NOT VISIBLE/AUTHORIZED FOR THE VIEWER STILL INFLUENCE INTELLIGENCE SHOWN TO THAT VIEWER?

This is an INVESTIGATION.

Do not assume a violation exists.

CORE RULE TO AUDIT
==================================================

For each intelligence surface, distinguish between:

A. SOURCE VISIBILITY

Would the viewer be authorized to see the underlying activity/data?

and:

B. DERIVED INFLUENCE

Can that underlying activity/data affect:

- whether something appears
- ranking
- score
- ordering
- recommendation eligibility
- recommendation strength
- counts
- summaries
- labels
- copy
- notifications
- email
- push
- badges
- contextual insights
- “friends are…” language
- mountain suggestions
- trip suggestions
- date suggestions

A surface can violate BL-85 even if it never directly renders the hidden trip.

Example:

A private trip is correctly absent from the UI, but still boosts Aspen from #5 to #1 in a recommendation list.

That is still hidden-data influence and must be reported.
