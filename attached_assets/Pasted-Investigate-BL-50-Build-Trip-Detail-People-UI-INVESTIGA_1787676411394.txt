Investigate BL-50 — Build Trip Detail People UI.

INVESTIGATION ONLY. Do not implement or modify anything.

- BL-58 self-RSVP is complete.

The goal of BL-50 is to make the Trip Detail People section coherent, compact, and mobile-friendly without rebuilding the underlying RSVP or attendance systems.

Audit the current Trip Detail People experience for:

- Organizer/owner
- Going participant
- Interested participant
- Pending invitee
- Declined participant
- Removed participant if relevant
- Non-participant / unauthorized viewer
- Public friend-trip viewer where applicable

For each state, document:
- what appears in the People section
- participant grouping/order
- status chips/labels
- participant date display
- setup/gear display if present
- organizer actions
- self-RSVP controls
- pending/declined handling
- join-request handling
- empty states
- mobile wrapping/spacing issues
- duplicate or redundant information

Pay particular attention to whether the current People section now has overlapping UI from BL-58 and BL-59 that should be consolidated visually.

Do NOT change the underlying behavior for:
- RSVP state transitions
- organizer management routes
- participant attendance dates
- invite/reinvite semantics
- join requests
- permissions
- notifications/activity
- trip privacy
- group-trip behavior

Determine the best mobile-first People-section structure.

The desired outcome should make it immediately obvious:
1. Who is Going
2. Who is Interested
3. Who is Pending
4. Who Declined, where appropriate
5. When each Going participant will actually be there, if they have personal dates
6. What actions are available to the organizer
7. What actions belong to the current participant versus the organizer

Recommend:
- grouping/order
- row structure
- labels/status presentation
- whether participant dates should appear inline or secondary
- whether setup/gear belongs in the row or elsewhere
- whether collapsed sections are useful
- whether Declined should be visually de-emphasized
- how Pending should be handled
- how many visible controls should appear per row on mobile
