==================================================
LOCKED PRODUCT STRUCTURE
==================================================

The redesigned Trips screen will eventually have three views:

MINE
“What am I doing?”

- My organizing trips
- My going trips
- My interested trips
- Pending trip invitations requiring my response
- Invitations are actionable inline
- Invitations do not appear in Both or Friends’

BOTH
“Where do my season and my friends’ season intersect?”

- My actual trips
- Relevant friend activity/opportunities
- Must reuse/share the same underlying relevance logic as Home Ideas
- Same-mountain/date overlap should generally annotate my trip rather than duplicate it
- No pending invitations
- Do not assume wishlist, availability, or friend attendance alone is sufficient relevance unless the existing Home Ideas logic says it is

FRIENDS’
“What are my friends doing?”

- Visible friend trips
- No invitations
- Same mountain + exact same date range should consolidate into a single trip-centric row
- Different date ranges remain separate
- Consolidation must preserve participant/status information correctly

All three views:
- always grouped by month
- no mountain filter
- mountain-led rows
- date right aligned
- no duplicated status labels

This audit does NOT implement any of this.

AUDIT 5 — FRIENDS’ TRIP CONSOLIDATION
==================================================

The future Friends’ view should consolidate rows when friends have:

SAME normalized mountain/resort
AND
SAME exact trip date range

Example:

Dana: Indy Basin, Jan 12–15, Going
Theo: Indy Basin, Jan 12–15, Going
Marcus: Indy Basin, Jan 12–15, Going

becomes one display row.

Investigate whether the current query/data shape supports this safely.

Determine:

1. Current Friends’ Trips query/projection.
2. Visibility/privacy filtering.
3. Resort normalization.
4. Date normalization.
5. Duplicate handling.
6. Whether multiple friends can point to the same underlying Trip versus separate trips with identical dates/resort.
7. Whether grouping those two cases together would be semantically safe.

IMPORTANT: investigate mixed participation states.

Example:

Dana — Going
Theo — Interested
Marcus — Going

Same mountain and exact dates.

Do NOT simply label the consolidated row “going.”

Report what status information exists and propose safe display-projection options that preserve the underlying meaning.

Also investigate organizer information and whether that matters to consolidation.
