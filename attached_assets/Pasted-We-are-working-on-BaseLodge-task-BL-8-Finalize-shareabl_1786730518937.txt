# 5. Month Groups Are Preferred, Not Absolute

Chronology remains:

**top to bottom in column 1 → top to bottom in column 2 → top to bottom in column 3**

Earlier trips must visually precede later trips.

The preference is to keep a month group together.

However, BL-8 says month groups should remain intact **whenever practical**.

That means an entire month must **not** become an indivisible block if doing so causes the fixed 4:5 card to fail.

For example:

If someone has 10 trips in January and 5 trips in February, keeping all 10 January trips in one column may produce a terrible or impossible layout.

The algorithm should be allowed to continue a month in the next column when necessary.

If a month is split, determine how the month label should behave.

Possible approaches might include:

* repeat `JAN` at the top of the continuation
* use a subtle continuation treatment
* another clean approach consistent with the Option 4 visual system

Recommend the simplest understandable treatment.
