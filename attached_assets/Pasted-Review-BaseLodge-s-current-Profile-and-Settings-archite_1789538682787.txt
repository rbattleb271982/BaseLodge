GOAL

IMPORTANT PRODUCT CONTEXT

Do not assume that every existing screen needs to survive.

We specifically want you to identify opportunities where BaseLodge may be better if:

- two destinations were consolidated;
- a standalone screen became a section, sheet, or contextual action;
- an edit workflow moved closer to where the information is displayed;
- Profile became more clearly a personal ski identity/dashboard;
- Settings became more clearly configuration/account management;
- duplicated information or controls were removed;
- navigation became simpler;
- an old route or UI concept no longer justified its existence.

These are PRODUCT OPPORTUNITIES, not defects. Clearly separate them from demonstrated bugs or inconsistencies.

Do not recommend consolidation merely because two surfaces share data. Contextual repetition may be useful.

7. Identify duplication and classify each instance as:

A. USEFUL CONTEXTUAL REPETITION
The same information appropriately appears in multiple contexts.

B. INCONSISTENT DUPLICATION
The same concept appears differently or has conflicting edit/ownership patterns.

C. REDUNDANT WORKFLOW
Multiple places effectively perform the same management job without a clear product reason.

D. OWNERSHIP MISMATCH
Information appears to belong to one product surface but must be managed somewhere conceptually unrelated.

E. LEGACY/PRESENTATION DEBT
A route, screen, control, or hierarchy appears to survive primarily because of historical implementation.

Do not call something redundant simply because it shares underlying data.
