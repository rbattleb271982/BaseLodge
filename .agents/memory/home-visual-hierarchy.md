Preserve the Round 11 G personal context and Next Trip structure. In the lower page, Needs You is the strongest action surface, Ideas is a lighter recommendation surface with J WHY chips, and Happening is the quietest, shortest M digest. Use differentiated fill, keyline strength, radius, and spacing rather than making all three sections look like equivalent cards.

**Why:** The approved convergence pass found that repeated rounded borders and full-width rules made requests, recommendations, and passive activity read as one continuous component stack.

**How to apply:** Keep 20px gutters and natural wrapping at 390px and true 360px. Preserve full interaction targets while making dismiss glyphs and passive metadata visually quieter. Do not change the locked section semantics, ordering, routes, or query behavior to achieve visual polish.
