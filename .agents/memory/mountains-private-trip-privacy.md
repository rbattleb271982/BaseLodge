---
name: Mountains private-trip privacy
description: Product privacy boundary for private trips in Mountains counts and social proof.
---

Private trips must never contribute to Mountains friend counts or social proof, including when the viewer is a participant or otherwise has another relationship to the trip.

**Why:** The product decision intentionally uses a stricter social-proof boundary than general trip access. Authorization to interact with a private trip does not authorize exposing its destination through Mountains aggregates.

**How to apply:** Filter private trips before any Mountains aggregation or social-proof derivation. Do not add owner, participant, friendship, invitation, or other relationship exceptions; broader visibility policy belongs to its dedicated authorization work.
